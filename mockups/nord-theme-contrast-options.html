Summary & Recommendations

Top Recommendations

  1. Option 3: White base + Frost-tinted Todo - Best overall balance

Key Changes for Options 2/3

  • Light base: #ffffff (Option 3) or keep #eceff4 (Option 2)
  • Dark base: #242933 (darker than current)
  • Todo column: rgba(94,129,172,0.12) light / rgba(136,192,208,0.1) dark
  • Add subtle border to all columns for definition
