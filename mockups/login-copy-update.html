Recommendation
Option 1

Option 1 — The Self-Awareness Hook is recommended because:

  • Leads with Pillar 1 — The self-awareness gap is the primary differentiator. "See yourself the way others do" immediately communicates the unique value that no competitor offers.
  • Emotionally engaging — It's a tagline that makes you stop and think. The best login page copy makes you curious, not informed. Information lives in the description.
  • Short and scannable — Six words. A returning user doesn't need to re-read it; a new user can absorb it in under a second.
  • Description covers the mechanism — "Analyzes your meetings, reveals your communication blind spots, and tracks your growth over time" explains the how without being a feature list.
  • Matches the product vision — The vision document says the emotional core is "we show you what you can't see about yourself." This tagline is that sentence, reframed as an invitation.

Runner-up: Option 7 (Hybrid) is a strong alternative if you want to pack more information into the description. It uses the same tagline but adds "Your AI meeting coach" to the description for category clarity. The trade-off is a slightly longer description line.
