Summary

Unlike Notes (long-living, frequently edited), Meetings are:

  • Date-centric (grouped by day)
  • Time-stamped (specific start times)
  • Write-once (recorded, analyzed, then archived)
  • Action-focused (extract tasks, then move on)

Option 1 (Daily Grouped List) is recommended because it naturally reflects how we think about meetings, prominently shows time, and clearly indicates today's active/recording meetings.
