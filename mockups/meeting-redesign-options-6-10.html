6

Two-Column Editor: Metadata Left, Transcript Right

Full-page editor split into two columns. Left column has meeting details (compact form). Right column is the transcript editor taking more space. Maximizes transcript visibility while keeping details accessible.

Cons

  • Two-column layout doesn't work well on mobile
  • Metadata sidebar takes space even when not needed
