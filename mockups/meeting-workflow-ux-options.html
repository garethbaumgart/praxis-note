Summary: Workflow UX Options

Option Best For Complexity
A. Wizard/Stepper New users, guided creation flow Medium
B. Single Page Reviewing completed meetings Low
C. Tab-Based Organized navigation Low
D. Live Recording Active meeting participation High
E. Card-Based Mobile-friendly, scannable Low

Recommendation: Consider Option A (Wizard) for the creation flow and Option B or E for viewing completed meetings. The wizard guides users through recording → analysis → actions, while the detail view lets them review anytime.
