Recommendation: Option 1

Option 1 (Extracted Title + Rich HTML Body with fade-out) provides the best balance of:

  • Clear hierarchy — title stands out without being intrusive
  • Rich formatting — users see their actual note structure at a glance
  • Graceful fallback — notes without headings just show the rich body
  • Consistent with existing design — keeps the card minimal and clean
  • Fade-out overflow — natural indicator that there's more content
