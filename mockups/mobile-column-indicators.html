Recommendation: Option 5 (Segmented Progress Bar)

The segmented bar is the most space-efficient option while still providing clear visual feedback. The color-coding connects it to the column theme, and the slightly taller active segment provides clear indication of the current position. It's also tappable for direct navigation.
