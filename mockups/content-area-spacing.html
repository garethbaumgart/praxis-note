Option 4: Balanced Increase — Both Axes + Wider Section Gaps Recommended
Container: max-w-7xl (1280px) • Padding: px-6/px-10 (24px/40px) • Vertical: py-8/py-10 (32px/40px) • Section gaps: mb-6→mb-8

Pros

  • Balanced breathing room on all sides — less busy
  • Section gaps increased too, reducing visual noise
  • Still keeps enough content density to be useful
  • Moderate change — not dramatically different

Cons

  • Slightly less content visible above fold
  • Requires updating all page containers consistently

Option 6: Spacious — Maximum Breathing Room
