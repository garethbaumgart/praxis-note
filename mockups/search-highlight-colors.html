Recommendation

Option 2 (Frost Cyan) is recommended because:

  • Subtle but visible - draws attention without being jarring
  • Works well in both light and dark modes
  • Doesn't conflict with tag colors (green) or status colors
  • Feels more modern and polished than traditional yellow highlights

Options to avoid:

  • Orange (Option 5) - conflicts with "In Progress" column indicator
  • Yellow (Option 1) - can feel outdated/aggressive
