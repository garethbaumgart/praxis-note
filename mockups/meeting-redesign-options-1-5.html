Meeting Redesign - Full Content Space

20 UX Options (1-5 shown here) — Each with Light & Dark themes

Goals: Match task input background colors, open meetings in full content space (like notes), improve layout.
See also: Options 6-10 | Options 11-15 | Options 16-20

1

Split Panel - List + Full Editor

★ Recommended

Meeting list on the left (collapsible sidebar), full-width editor on the right. Matches how notes work but keeps the list accessible. Clicking a meeting fills the content area with the editor.
